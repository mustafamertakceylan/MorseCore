# Learning

![Learning workspace]({{MEDIA_URL}}/wiki/learning.en.png)

*The image shows macOS; the Windows layout may differ.*

Expand **Learning** in the sidebar. Its four pages provide a reference alphabet, recognition exercises, sending practice and a radio glossary. You can use these tools offline without a radio licence or connected radio.

## Look up a symbol

Open **Morse alphabet** and select **Latin / Turkish**, **Russian** or **Arabic**. Search for a character or its Morse pattern. Select a symbol to hear it; **Stop** ends playback. The alphabet selection concerns conversion, independently of the interface language.

## Recognise Morse

1. Open **Exercises** and choose letters, digits, words, Q codes, call signs or prosigns.
2. Select **Start**, read or play the current signal, and enter your answer.
3. Submit it, or skip to see the expected answer.
4. Follow the correct, incorrect and skipped totals. Use **Review mistakes** to revisit missed items.

These are recognition exercises; they do not check a radio operator's licence or award a qualification.

## Practise sending

Open **Sending practice**, choose the alphabet and target WPM, and optionally enter reference text. Focus the large key area, then hold and release Space, or use the pointer. Space in a text field continues to type normally.

Choose **Finish and review** to review the decoded text, timing, rhythm and optional text accuracy. Interpretation depends on the selected target speed; uneven spacing can produce another letter. A new attempt replaces the previous result. Leaving the page releases the key and stops its tone.

## Find radio terms

The **Radio glossary** contains Q codes, abbreviations, joined prosigns and the phonetic alphabet. Search or filter by category, then read an explanation and example. For contact preparation rather than exercises, see [Amateur radio](radio.md).
