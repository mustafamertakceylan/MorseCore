# Start with MorseCore

MorseCore is a free desktop app for converting text, listening to Morse, and preparing and logging amateur radio contacts. Your messages and contact records stay on your computer.

## Find what you need

| What you want to do | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Convert text to Morse or Morse to text | **Converter** |
| Convert text files individually or in batches | **Files** |
| Play Morse, export WAV, read text aloud or decode audio | **Audio** |
| Look up the alphabet, practise and try sending | **Learning** |
| Set up stations, prepare messages, log contacts and view the map | **Amateur radio** |
| Change language, theme, separator, save locations and update preferences | **Settings** |

![English converter]({{MEDIA_URL}}/converter.en.png)

## Your first three steps

1. [Install the Windows or Mac version](installation.md).
2. [Convert your first message](first-steps.md).
3. Read the [Audio guide](audio.md) to listen to Morse or install an offline voice.

Menu language, conversion alphabet and speech language are separate choices. Arabic menus mirror from right to left; Morse fields remain left to right. Light/dark themes and reduced motion are in **Settings**.

Packages are available for Windows x64 and Apple Silicon Macs. **There is no Linux desktop app yet.** The app's menus and voice choices cover five languages; this first Wiki is in English and Turkish.
